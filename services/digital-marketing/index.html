Services

Digital Marketing

Content and campaigns that build an audience.

Discuss your digital marketing needs

Publishing regularly is not the same as giving people a reason to pay attention.

I help businesses connect what they know and offer with what their audience wants to understand—through useful content, focused campaigns, and a clear plan for getting both in front of the right people.

Not just more activity to report at the end of the month.

Approach

A clear purpose. A reason to come back.

Your website, search content, social posts, and emails should support each other—not operate as separate departments with occasional diplomatic contact.

I bring the message, content, and distribution together around your business goals and your audience’s interests.

The focus is relevance: reaching people with something worth their time, then giving them a reason to stay connected.

What I do

From publishing content to building an audience.

Digital Marketing Strategy

Give the activity a direction.

Clarify who you want to reach, what you want to be known for, and what you want your marketing to achieve.

I help define the priorities, messages, channels, and content themes that guide the work. So “we should post something” does not become the entire strategy.

Content Strategy & Creation

Have something useful to say.

Develop content around the questions your audience asks, the problems they face, and the expertise your business can contribute.

From articles and newsletters to website copy and campaign assets, I shape ideas into content with a clear purpose and a recognisable voice. The internet is not suffering from a shortage of words.

SEO & Website Content

Help people find you—and understand why they should stay.

Connect the language your audience uses with clear, well-structured website content.

I bring search intent, topic planning, and on-page copy together so your pages answer relevant questions and explain your offer. Being found is useful. Making sense once someone arrives is also part of the job.

Social Media Content

Build a presence with a point of view.

Create social content that reflects your expertise, speaks to your audience, and gives people something to engage with.

I develop themes, editorial calendars, copy, and visual briefs that keep the work connected. Consistency should mean more than appearing every Tuesday.

Campaign Planning & Content

Give the message somewhere to go.

Bring launches, offers, events, and ideas into focused campaigns with a clear audience and intended response.

I plan the message, content sequence, channels, and next steps—so the announcement is the beginning of the campaign, not its most organised moment.

Email & Newsletters

Make the inbox visit worthwhile.

Develop emails that give readers a clear reason to open, read, and respond.

From newsletters and launch sequences to follow-up communication, I help structure the message around what matters to the recipient. “Just checking in” may occasionally need a stronger supporting argument.

Content Repurposing & Distribution

Get more from the thinking you have already done.

Turn a substantial idea into connected content for different formats and channels.

An article can become a newsletter, a series of posts, a visual explainer, or a useful resource. The aim is to adapt the idea to each setting—not copy the same paragraph into five places and call it distribution.

Measurement & Review

Look beyond whether it went out.

Set clear objectives and review the available evidence against them.

I help bring performance findings into editorial and campaign decisions: what attracted attention, what prompted a response, and what needs to change. A full publishing calendar is evidence of a full publishing calendar.

Process

How we work together.

  1. 01

    Understand the starting point

    Explore your business goals, audience, existing content, and current channels. Identify what is working, what is missing, and what is taking effort without a clear purpose.

  2. 02

    Set the priorities

    Agree on the audiences, themes, channels, and objectives to focus on. Decide what progress should look like before deciding how much to publish.

  3. 03

    Build the plan

    Develop the editorial calendar or campaign structure, including key messages, formats, distribution, and responsibilities. Make it workable with the time and resources available.

  4. 04

    Create and coordinate

    Develop the content and campaign materials, adapting them for each channel while keeping the central message consistent.

  5. 05

    Review and refine

    Look at the results, audience responses, and gaps. Use what emerges to improve the next round—not simply produce the previous month again with different dates.

Scope

What this can include.

Depending on your needs, an engagement can bring together:

  • Digital marketing and content reviews
  • Audience and channel planning
  • Content strategy and editorial calendars
  • SEO topic research and on-page content
  • Website and landing-page copy
  • Articles, thought leadership, and long-form content
  • Social media content and visual briefs
  • Campaign messaging and content sequences
  • Email newsletters and follow-up sequences
  • Lead magnets and downloadable resources
  • Content repurposing and distribution plans
  • Performance summaries and recommendations

Whether you are establishing your presence, launching an offer, or bringing more direction to existing activity, the goal is not simply to publish more.

It is to make your expertise visible, your message relevant, and the next step clear.
