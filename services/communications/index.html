Services

Communications

Internal and external messages that are clear, timely and acted on.

Discuss your communication needs

A message can reach everyone and still leave people unsure what it means—or what to do next.

I help organisations communicate with purpose, whether they are explaining a change, aligning teams, or speaking to clients and stakeholders.

The work starts with what your audience needs to understand, not just what your organisation wants to say.

Approach

Clear on the purpose. Relevant to the audience.

Your employees, leaders, clients, and partners do not all need the same information. They bring different expectations, questions, and levels of context.

I work with you to understand those differences and shape communication around them: what needs to be said, who needs to hear it, when it will be useful, and how it should reach them.

The focus is clarity: helping people understand what matters and what happens next.

What I do

From getting the message out to helping it land.

Give communication a clear direction

Decide what the communication needs to achieve before choosing a format or channel.

I help define the purpose, audience, key messages, and intended response—so each piece of communication has a job to do.

Help people understand change

Explain what is changing without overlooking what it means for the people affected.

I develop communication that connects the reasons for change with its practical implications: what will be different, what remains the same, and where people can find support.

Support leaders and managers

Help the people delivering a message communicate it clearly and confidently.

From leadership updates and talking points to manager briefings and team discussion guides, I create materials that support consistent communication without scripting every conversation.

Make complex information accessible

Bring structure to information that is technical, detailed, or difficult to explain.

I work with subject-matter experts to identify what audiences need to know, preserve the important detail, and express it in language they can use.

Connect internal and external messages

Keep what you say to the outside world connected to what people hear inside the organisation.

I help align the central message while adapting the emphasis, detail, and tone for employees, clients, partners, and other stakeholders.

Build in space for a response

Treat communication as an exchange, not a finished deliverable.

I help create ways for people to ask questions, raise concerns, and share feedback—and use those responses to identify what needs further explanation.

Process

How we work together.

  1. 01

    Understand the situation

    Explore what you need to communicate, why it matters now, and who it affects. Review the existing context, sensitivities, and communication gaps.

  2. 02

    Define the message

    Agree on the purpose, key points, and intended response. Separate what people need to know now from what can follow later.

  3. 03

    Plan the delivery

    Choose the channels, formats, timing, and people best placed to deliver the message. Clarify responsibilities and approvals before production begins.

  4. 04

    Develop the content

    Create and refine the communication, working with your teams to check accuracy, relevance, and tone. Adapt it for different audiences without losing the central message.

  5. 05

    Listen and refine

    Review the questions, feedback, and available evidence of understanding or action. Address gaps and adjust subsequent communication.

Scope

What this can include.

Depending on your needs, an engagement can bring together:

  • Communication reviews and audience mapping
  • Internal and external communication plans
  • Key messages and organisational narratives
  • Change announcements and employee updates
  • Leadership messages, speeches, and talking points
  • Manager briefings, FAQs, and discussion guides
  • Client and stakeholder communication
  • Website, newsletter, and editorial content
  • Editorial calendars and content coordination
  • Feedback gathering and communication reviews

Say what matters. Make the next step clear.

Whether you are communicating a significant change or improving everyday updates, the aim is the same: give people the information they need, at the right time, with a clear sense of what it means for them.
