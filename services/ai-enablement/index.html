Services

AI Enablement

Helping teams understand AI and use it in their real work, beyond the rollout.

Discuss your AI enablement needs

Introducing AI is one thing. Making it useful in everyday work takes more.

People need to understand what AI can help with, where its limits are, and what they remain responsible for. They also need time and support to apply that understanding to the work in front of them.

I help organisations connect AI adoption with clear communication, practical learning, and better ways of working.

Approach

Built around your people. Grounded in their work.

Your teams bring different skills, expectations, and concerns to AI. A single training session or a standard set of prompts cannot address all of them.

I work with you to understand where people are starting, what their roles require, and where AI could make a meaningful difference. That understanding shapes the communication, learning, and practical support they receive.

The focus is usefulness: helping people make informed decisions about when and how to use AI.

What I do

From access to everyday use.

Make AI understandable

Give people a clear explanation of what is changing, why it matters, and what it means for their role.

I turn technical concepts and organisational plans into communication people can understand and act on—with room for questions, concerns, and honest discussion about limitations.

Connect AI to real tasks

Move beyond demonstrations to the work people actually do.

Together, we identify relevant tasks, examine where AI might help, and develop practical examples around existing workflows. The aim is not to use AI everywhere. It is to use it where it serves a clear purpose.

Build confidence and judgement

Help people assess the output, not just generate it.

Learning activities focus on asking useful questions, providing context, checking results, and recognising when human expertise or review is needed.

Make responsible use practical

Bring expectations out of policy documents and into everyday decisions.

I help translate your organisation’s AI guidance into clear examples, accessible resources, and role-specific communication—so people understand what they can use, what they should protect, and when to seek advice.

Support managers through the change

Give managers the clarity to support their teams.

That includes explaining expectations, making space for learning, responding to uncertainty, and discussing how AI may change tasks without losing sight of quality or accountability.

Keep learning after launch

Create ways to learn from what happens in practice.

Team feedback, shared examples, and regular reviews help identify what is useful, what is unclear, and where further support is needed.

Process

How we work together.

  1. 01

    Understand the starting point

    Explore your goals, existing tools, team readiness, and current ways of working. Identify the questions and constraints the work needs to address.

  2. 02

    Choose a practical focus

    Agree on the teams, tasks, and use cases to prioritise. Define what useful progress would look like before developing the support around it.

  3. 03

    Develop the support

    Create the communication, learning sessions, workflow guidance, and resources your teams need. Keep them relevant to people’s roles and aligned with your organisation’s expectations.

  4. 04

    Apply it to real work

    Give teams opportunities to practise, ask questions, and review results using relevant tasks. Use their experience to refine the approach.

  5. 05

    Review and adapt

    Look at what people are using, where they are struggling, and what has changed in the work. Identify the next steps for sustained support.

Scope

What this can include.

Depending on your needs, an engagement can bring together:

  • Team readiness conversations and stakeholder interviews
  • AI adoption and change communication
  • Role-specific workshops and guided practice
  • Use-case discovery and workflow mapping
  • Practical guides, FAQs, and learning resources
  • Manager briefings and discussion guides
  • Communication that makes AI policies easier to apply
  • Feedback and review sessions to inform the next phase

Make AI part of the work—not another initiative alongside it.

Whether you are preparing to introduce AI or working out why an existing rollout has not translated into everyday use, the starting point is the same: understand what people need to do differently, and help them do it.
