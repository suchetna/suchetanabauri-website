Services

Thought Leadership

Turning leaders’ expertise into articles and posts people want to read.

Discuss your thought leadership

You have experience, ideas, and a perspective shaped by doing the work. Getting that into a clear, compelling piece of writing is another task—and usually the one left until there is time.

I help leaders turn what they know into articles, posts, and other content that sounds like them and gives readers something worth considering.

Not just confirmation that they attended a conference and found it insightful.

Approach

Your expertise. Your point of view. Clearly expressed.

The most useful material is not always sitting in a draft. It may be in a conversation, a decision you made, a pattern you keep noticing, or something your industry accepts that you think deserves a closer look.

I work with you to draw out those ideas, examine the argument, and shape them into writing with substance and character.

The focus is perspective: making clear not only what you know, but what you make of it—and why someone else should care.

What I do

From something worth saying to something worth reading.

Thought Leadership Strategy

Decide what you want to contribute.

Identify the subjects where your experience, interests, and audience needs meet.

I help define your editorial focus, core themes, and point of view—so your content builds a connected body of work rather than a record of whatever was trending that morning.

Idea Development & Interviews

Find the thinking behind the talking points.

Use focused conversations to uncover the observations, experiences, and questions that could become strong pieces.

I ask questions, make connections, and help distinguish a promising subject from an actual argument. “AI is changing everything” is a topic. We still need to know what you think.

Articles & Opinion Pieces

Give the argument room to develop.

Turn your expertise into structured, well-supported writing for your website, LinkedIn, Medium, or a relevant publication.

I help establish the central argument, bring in examples and evidence, and build a narrative readers can follow—without smoothing away everything that makes the perspective yours.

LinkedIn & Social Posts

Make the point without the preamble.

Develop posts that bring your observations and expertise into a format suited to the channel.

I write and edit for clarity, pace, and voice, giving each post a reason to exist beyond maintaining visibility. Not every professional experience needs a dramatic opening and a life lesson.

Ghostwriting & Voice Development

Sound like yourself on a particularly clear day.

Translate your thinking into writing that reflects how you speak, what you value, and how you see your field.

I work from interviews, notes, recordings, or rough drafts to develop a consistent voice. You stay involved in the ideas and approvals. You do not have to spend your Sunday negotiating with the opening paragraph.

Research & Editorial Development

Give the perspective something solid to stand on.

Strengthen the writing with relevant research, examples, and careful qualification.

I help separate evidence from assumption, check the claims, and identify where the argument needs more work. Confidence is a tone, not a source.

Newsletters & Editorial Series

Build a conversation over time.

Develop recurring content around a clear editorial purpose.

I help plan themes, shape individual editions, and connect ideas across a series—giving readers a reason to return without asking you to discover a completely new professional identity every fortnight.

Repurposing & Publication Support

Let a strong idea travel.

Adapt substantial pieces into posts, newsletter editions, talking points, or publication pitches.

Each version keeps the central thinking while giving it a form suited to its audience. Shortening an article is sometimes useful. Removing every third paragraph is not an editorial strategy.

Process

How we work together.

  1. 01

    Establish the focus

    Explore your expertise, audience, goals, and existing content. Clarify what you want to be known for and where you have something meaningful to contribute.

  2. 02

    Draw out the ideas

    Use interviews, notes, and discussion to uncover your perspective. Identify the argument, supporting examples, and questions that need further investigation.

  3. 03

    Shape and draft

    Develop the structure and write the piece, bringing your thinking together with the context readers need. Keep the language clear and the voice recognisably yours.

  4. 04

    Challenge and refine

    Review the draft together for accuracy, substance, and tone. Strengthen weak points, remove unnecessary detail, and check that the writing says what you actually mean.

  5. 05

    Prepare and extend

    Finalise the piece for its intended channel. Where useful, develop supporting posts, summaries, or follow-up ideas so the thinking can reach beyond a single publication.

Scope

What this can include.

Depending on your needs, an engagement can bring together:

  • Thought leadership positioning and editorial themes
  • Existing-content and voice reviews
  • Expert interviews and idea-development sessions
  • Bylined articles and opinion pieces
  • Executive and founder ghostwriting
  • LinkedIn articles and social posts
  • Research-led explainers and commentary
  • Newsletters and editorial series
  • Editing and development of existing drafts
  • Publication pitches and article proposals
  • Content repurposing and supporting posts
  • Editorial calendars and ongoing writing support

Make your experience worth reading.

You do not need an opinion on every headline. You need a clear perspective on the subjects where your experience adds something.

I help bring that perspective into the open—with writing that respects your voice and your reader’s time.
