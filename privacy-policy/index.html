Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

01. Who we are

This website is owned and operated by Suchetana Bauri, an independent AI enablement consultant, under the brand “suchetanabauri.com”, based in Hyderabad, Telangana, India.

For any privacy-related enquiries, you can contact me directly at: privacy@suchetanabauri.com.

This Privacy Policy explains how I collect, use, and protect personal information when you visit this website, subscribe to content, or work with me. It is written to align with applicable Indian law (including the Information Technology Act, 2000, the SPDI Rules, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023) and other privacy regulations where relevant.

02. What information I collect

Information you send me

If you use the contact form, I receive your name, email address, organisation (if you add it), the topic you choose and your message. If you email me or message me through LinkedIn or WhatsApp, I receive whatever you choose to send. The website has no sign-ups or booking tools.

Information collected automatically

When you visit, this website uses Google Analytics to collect information about how the site is used. This includes the pages you view, how long you stay, how you arrived (for example, from a search engine or a link), your approximate location (city or country), and your browser and device type. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. My hosting provider also keeps standard server logs, which include IP addresses, for security and to keep the site running.

Note on sensitive data: I do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as financial details, health data, or information about minors through this website.

03. How I use your information

I use your information for specific, legitimate purposes:

  • To reply to enquiries you send me through the contact form, by email, LinkedIn or WhatsApp.
  • To understand which pages and articles people find useful, so I can improve the site’s content and navigation.
  • To keep this website secure and working properly.
  • To comply with legal obligations or resolve disputes.

The legal basis for processing depends on context: taking steps at your request before a consulting engagement, or my legitimate interest in running a secure and useful professional website.

04. Cookies and analytics

Google Analytics sets cookies (small files stored in your browser) so it can recognise repeat visits and measure how the site is used. The information is aggregated in reports; I do not use it to identify individual visitors, and I do not use it for advertising.

Google processes this data as my service provider. You can read how Google uses it at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or opt out of Google Analytics on every website with Google’s opt-out browser add-on. The site works fully without cookies.

05. How information is kept

I retain personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy:

Enquiries and Contact Records

Standard contact forms and initial email correspondence are retained for up to 2 years to facilitate business relationships, unless we enter a strategic consulting agreement.

Project/Engagement Records

Client consulting deliverables, legal agreement details, and necessary project performance logs are archived securely in line with tax, statutory reporting, and legal compliance mandates.

06. How information is shared

I do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information to third parties.

To operate effectively, I share structured data with specific trusted service categories:

  • Technical infrastructure hosts and database providers.
  • Form processing: messages sent through the contact form are delivered to my inbox by Web3Forms, which processes them only to deliver them.
  • Email marketing and newsletter software.
  • Professional consulting schedulers and calendar tools.
  • Professional legal, bookkeeping, or administrative advisors when strictly required.

I may also share information to comply with valid legal processes, protect intellectual property rights, preserve safety standards, or execute necessary business ownership transfers.

07. International transfers

Because I utilise specific cloud infrastructure, email deployment platforms, and calendar services based globally, your information may be processed outside of your home country. I ensure my technical processors maintain appropriate safety parameters, standard model clauses, and robust data protection standards.

08. Your rights and choices

You hold full authority over how your personal details are processed. Depending on your jurisdiction of residence, you may request:

  • Access to, or a portable copy of, your personal records.
  • Correction of inaccurate, incomplete, or out-of-date information.
  • Deletion of your personal records from my business infrastructure.
  • Withdrawal of any voluntary consent given for updates or analytics.

To exercise any of these options, please reach out via email: privacy@suchetanabauri.com. I will respond to valid legal requests within the statutory timeframe.

09. Data Security

I implement reasonable, industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical security measures (including secure SSL transmission and constrained access controls) to prevent unauthorized use, alteration, or disclosure. However, please note that no internet transmission protocol is completely risk-free.

10. Children's privacy

My professional consulting services and online content are created solely for adults. I do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of 18. If you believe a child has shared information on this website, please let me know and I will remove it swiftly.

11. Changes to this policy

I may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect shifts in my professional practices, legal mandates, or technical architectures. Any revisions will be accompanied by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this document. I encourage you to review this page regularly.
