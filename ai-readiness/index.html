        How this check works

        Scoring. Each answer scores 0 to 3, and each area adds up two questions, out of 6. The total, out of 36, places you in one of four levels: Starting (0–12), Exploring (13–24), Scaling (25–30) or Leading (31–36). These are fixed rules, so the same answers always get the same score.

        The written report. The summary, notes and first moves come from a set of recommendations I wrote for each area and level. Where available, an AI model (an open model, GPT-OSS, run by Groq) tailors them to your answers; it is instructed to use only what you told it, and the provider does not use your answers to train its models. Treat the suggestions as a starting point, not as professional or legal advice.

        Your data. Your answers are kept for 24 hours so the full report can be sent, then deleted. Nothing is stored long term unless you ask for the report by email; then your name, email, organisation and scores are kept so I can follow up. Please don’t enter confidential information.

        Limits. It reflects one person’s view, from twelve questions. Readiness varies a lot between teams, which is why a proper assessment talks to more people.
